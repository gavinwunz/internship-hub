## ✨ Why this repo

There are dozens of great internship resources scattered across GitHub, but they're fragmented — a job list here, a resume template there, an interview guide somewhere else. **The Internship Hub stitches the best of them into one map** so you can go from *"I need an internship"* to *applying* without 40 open tabs.

Every link below is:
- ✅ **Actively maintained** (or a stable, evergreen classic)
- ✅ **Free & open source**
- ✅ **Genuinely used** by people who landed offers

## 🌱 Build a portfolio via open source

No internship experience yet? **Merged PRs on real projects are the best proof of skill.** These help you find beginner-friendly issues:

| Resource | What it does |
|---|---|
| **[MunGell/awesome-for-beginners](https://github.com/MunGell/awesome-for-beginners)** | Curated list of projects with beginner-friendly issues. |

| **[goodfirstissue.dev](https://goodfirstissue.dev/)** | Aggregator that surfaces fresh, approachable issues. |
| **[firsttimersonly.com](https://www.firsttimersonly.com/)** | The gentle on-ramp explainer for your very first PR. |
| **Any repo `/contribute` page** | Append `/contribute` to a repo URL (e.g. `github.com/nodejs/node/contribute`) for a maintainer-curated starter list. |

> Start small: typo fixes and docs count. Consistency and etiquette beat one flashy PR.

## 🤝 Networking & cold outreach

The hidden half of internship hunting:

- **Referrals** move your application to the top of the pile — a warm intro beats 50 cold applies.
- **Cold email/DM** recruiters and engineers with a *specific*, short, personalized ask.
- **LinkedIn**: keep it current, follow target companies, and engage before you ask.
- **Career fairs & Discord communities** (e.g. CS Careers, ColorStack, university CS servers) are where postings and referrals circulate first.

## 🗺️ A 6-week game plan

```
Week 1  ── Set up: star trackers, watch for new postings, polish Jake's Resume
Week 2  ── DSA foundations: NeetCode roadmap + easy Blind 75
Week 3  ── Ship 1–2 open-source PRs for your portfolio & GitHub green squares
Week 4  ── Apply in volume: 10–15 quality applications from the live trackers
Week 5  ── Behavioral prep (STAR stories) + start reaching out for referrals
Week 6  ── Mock interviews, review company-specific questions, iterate & repeat
```

*Applying early and often — the day a posting drops — is the single biggest advantage. The trackers exist to make that fast.*

## 🤔 FAQ

**Q: Summer 2026 or 2027?**
It's August 2026 — apply for **Summer 2027**. Many top companies open apps July–September, so you may already be late for a few; apply now anyway.

**Q: Do I need system design for an internship?**
Rarely. Prioritize DSA + behavioral. System design is a bonus and matters more for new-grad/full-time.

**Q: I have no experience — what first?**
Jake's Resume → 2–3 open-source PRs (portfolio) → NeetCode roadmap → start applying. Don't wait until you feel "ready."

**Q: How many should I apply to?**
As many quality applications as you can sustain. Response rates are low; volume + speed + referrals win.
