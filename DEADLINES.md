> ### ⚠️ Read this first
> **Almost no top tech firm publishes a hard deadline — they hire *rolling*.** A posting can quietly close within *weeks* once enough strong applicants apply. The dates below are **compiled from third-party trackers and hiring guides, not official confirmations**, and several are *estimated from past cycles*. **Always verify on the company's official careers page**, and treat "apply the day it opens" as the real strategy. When in doubt: **earlier is always better.**

## ✅ How to use this timeline

1. **Star & watch the [live trackers](README.md#-live-internship-trackers-start-here)** — they post exact reqs the day they drop.
2. **Apply within the first 1–2 weeks** of any posting. Rolling means late = rejected-by-default.
3. **Prioritize by the "Closing soonest" table** above, then work down.
4. **Verify every date on the official careers page** before relying on it — these are estimates.
5. **Prep in parallel:** you can't wait until you're "ready." See [interview prep](README.md#-interview-preparation).
